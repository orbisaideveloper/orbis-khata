Send your request

Use your registered email to request deletion of your Khata Boi account and associated personal and owned business data, or specify selected data. Include your registered email/Orbis ID and relevant company/scope. If you cannot access the registered mailbox, ask for ownership recovery. Never send passwords or OTPs. Opening an email composer does not send a request; press Send in your mail app.

Verification and processing

Support verifies identity, ownership and shared-account impact before acting. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and explain any delay. Scope includes account/profile data, owned business records and linked statement access under service control; do not create new records while confirmed deletion is being processed. Specific legal/security retention, restricted data and expiry are explained. Deactivation alone does not fulfill deletion.

Backups and other products

Backups that cannot be selectively edited remain restricted until their scheduled expiry, which support will explain; deletions must be reapplied before active restore. Copies independently exported to others are outside service control. Khata-only deletion does not automatically delete other Orbis products. Support confirms whether you also request shared Orbis identity deletion and explains its impact.
