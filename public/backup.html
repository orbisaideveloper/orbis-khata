Google Drive Backup and Restore

Optional Android backup is in preparation. When available, it will let you authorize Khata to save an encrypted copy of your local accounts in the app-specific folder of a Google Drive account you choose.

Khata will request access to its own backup folder. When account-linked recovery is available, Khata will store the confirmed Google email and an encrypted copy of the recovery key with the verified ORBIS account. Restore uses the original ORBIS account and the same Google account. Older backups may need their original key once.
