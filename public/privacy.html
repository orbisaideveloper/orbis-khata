Khata Boi Privacy Policy

Khata Boi (com.orbisaideveloper.khata) is provided by Orbis AI Developer. Updated: 7 November 2026. Contact: orbisaideveloper@gmail.com

Information and purpose

Name, email/login identifier, credentials and account IDs support account creation, authentication and recovery. Optional profile fields include address, alternate phone, date of birth and gender. A phone field is processed only when the relevant service saves it; the current sign-up adapter forwards name, email and password. Passwords are not stored in local session storage.

Business records

Companies, party contacts, items, stock, sales, purchases, expenses, receipts, payments, bills and ledgers support bookkeeping, reports, synchronization and statement sharing. Lottery bookkeeping includes ticket stock, returns, commissions and prize adjustments. Enter other people’s information only with appropriate authority.

Storage and service providers

Central Orbis services process account/profile data; Khata services process business records. Render and Supabase provide hosting/database infrastructure. Network infrastructure may process IP addresses, request timing and operational/security logs. Devices store session tokens, preferences and operational local data. Share files may remain in app cache; downloaded/exported copies remain at destinations you choose. Data is not all stored only on your phone.

Sharing and permissions

Business data is not public by default. Invited statement recipients and apps/people you select for sharing receive the information you share. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. Supported browser contact selection reads the selected phone number, not the entire address book. The current Android manifest requests Internet permission, not broad contacts, SMS, location, camera or microphone access; no advertising or third-party analytics SDK is included.

Security and retention

HTTPS and authenticated company access protect service access, without a guarantee of absolute security. Records remain while needed to provide the account/bookkeeping service; corrections may retain audit/reversal history. Request access, correction or deletion using the contact above. After identity/ownership verification, we aim to finish deletion within 30 days, explaining delays and any specific legal/security retention reason, dataset and expiry. Deactivation alone is not deletion.

Backups, scope and children

If backups cannot be selectively edited, access remains restricted until scheduled expiry; applicable expiry is explained during the request. Confirmed deletions must be reapplied before restoring data to active service. Uninstall/logout does not delete server records or independent recipients’ copies. Khata-only requests do not automatically delete other Orbis products; shared-identity scope is confirmed first. The app is designed for business users, not children. Changes to this policy update the date above.

Availability checks may request a public health endpoint without account credentials or business data. Hosting/network providers may still record request IP addresses and timestamps in operational logs. These checks do not save accounting records or guarantee uninterrupted availability.
